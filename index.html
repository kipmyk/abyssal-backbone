        How to Use This Simulator

        • Viewports (Split / Seafloor / Globe): Switch views in the top bar. Press F or click Focus (F) to collapse panels and maximize 3D inspection.
        • BGP-4 Path Inspector: Select origin and destination Cable Landing Stations (or click stations on the globe) to inspect AS_PATH, LOCAL_PREF, MED, and real-time detour paths.
        • Fault Injection & Before/After Comparison: Run any curated fault scenario or toggle Globe Click: Fault / Repair to sever/repair trunks directly. The Before/After card compares capacity, RTT, and every impacted BGP corridor.
        • Cable Anatomy & C-OTDR: Inspect individual trunks to see C-OTDR localization uncertainty, seabed depth/pressure, and live ROV splicing stages.
